THE TAKEAWAY

You can’t erase your footprint. You can choose what it says about you.

KNOW

what telemetry and micro-signals are gathered silently in the background.

LIMIT

what you share and allow. Compartmentalize identities and practice data minimization.

QUESTION

anything that is “free”. Recognize that your attention is being monetized.

“The question isn’t whether we’re tracked. It’s who controls what’s built from it.”

SPEAKER NOTES (Speaker B · 30s) “You cannot completely erase your digital footprint in the 21st century. But you can actively control what it says about you. Know what is collected. Because ultimately, the defining question of our generation isn't whether we're tracked—it's who controls what is built from it.”
