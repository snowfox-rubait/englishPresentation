02 · TELEMETRY

What gets logged about you

Location

GPS coordinates, connected Wi-Fi networks, Bluetooth beacons, daily travel routes.

Searches

Queries, medical worries, curiosities, and late-night private questions.

Purchases

Credit card categories, items bought, brand loyalty, and items abandoned in cart.

Contacts

Call frequencies, synced address books, interaction reciprocity, and proximity.

Device Info

Battery health, screen resolution, browser extensions, audio hardware fingerprints.

Micro-Signals

Scroll velocity, hover duration, deleted draft comments, and insomnia scroll hours.

Micro-signals require zero voluntary sharing: lingering over a post without clicking still logs interest.

SPEAKER NOTES (Speaker A · 45s) “What kinds of data are actually stored? Location, searches, purchases, and device specs are standard. But look closely at tile 6: Micro-Signals. Modern social media apps and web platforms track telemetry down to the millisecond. If you pause over an image in your feed for three seconds without clicking 'Like', that dwell time is recorded as a micro-signal of interest. If you type a response and delete it, the hesitation is noted. If you scroll between 2 AM and 4 AM, the algorithm flags sleep disruption and possible emotional vulnerability. Each data point seems trivial on its own. Bound together, they reconstruct your daily life.”
