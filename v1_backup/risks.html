SPEAKER B · about 60 seconds “Why should you care? Three reasons. Profiling: ads and offers are shaped by a profile you have never seen. Scams: leaked details make phishing messages look personal and convincing. Doxxing: someone collects scattered public details and publishes them to harass or threaten a person. The technique behind the last two is called OSINT, open-source intelligence. It simply means collecting information from public sources, like social media, public records and old accounts. A username, an old post and a tagged photo look harmless alone. Combined, they reveal a lot.” OPTIONAL 20-SECOND DEMO (cut it if you are over time): show a username search on screen. Use only a teammate’s own public accounts, with their permission. Never search for a classmate or teacher without their consent.
