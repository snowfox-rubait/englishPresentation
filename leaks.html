06 · PERMANENT RISK

When it leaks

You can change a password. You can’t change your NID or your face.

KYC & Age Checks

Data Honeypots

Every bank, crypto exchange, and app demanding national identity documents and verification selfies creates an attractive honeypot for cybercriminals.

Permanent Breaches

No Reset Button

Credentials can be revoked, but leaked national IDs, biometric selfies, and birth records are permanent. Major breaches (e.g. Equifax, national ID exposures) leak lifetime identifiers.

Identity + Telemetry

Targeted Exploitation

When leaked government IDs are synthesized with browsing telemetry, attackers launch devastating spear-phishing, SIM swaps, financial fraud, extortion, and doxxing attacks.

SPEAKER NOTES (Speaker B · 45s) “The ultimate risk of digital footprints isn't just annoying ads—it is permanent vulnerability when data inevitably leaks. Today, countless services demand KYC: uploads of your passport, national ID card, and a facial biometric selfie. These corporate servers become giant data honeypots. Here is the fundamental difference: if someone steals your password, you can change it in 30 seconds. But you cannot rotate your national ID number, your date of birth, or your face. Once leaked in a breach—such as the massive Equifax breach or public government portal exposures—that data is out forever. When criminals marry leaked identity documents with your browsing habits, it enables flawless identity theft, SIM-swap attacks, and targeted extortion.”
