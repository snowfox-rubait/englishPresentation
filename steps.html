What you can do: 6 Actionable Habits

1. Audit Yourself

Search your full name, phone numbers, and handles across search engines to see your public attack surface.

2. Lock Down Settings

Make social profiles private, disable ad personalization, and turn off background location permissions.

3. Limit App Access

Enforce strict least privilege: does a calculator or flashlight app need your contacts or microphone? Deny it.

4. Separate Identities

Use unique email aliases (e.g. SimpleLogin) and distinct usernames for banking, social media, and newsletters.

5. Share Less ID

Practice data minimization: challenge unnecessary ID requests and avoid uploading verification documents.

6. Delete Old Accounts

Unused forums and abandoned apps still leak credentials during breaches. Regularly purge dormant accounts.

The mindset behind all six is OPSEC (Operational Security): think about what you expose before you post.
