SPEAKER B · about 60 seconds “Here is how it works. You use a free app or website. Trackers such as cookies, browser fingerprinting and app permissions record what you do. Fingerprinting even works without cookies, because it identifies your browser and device. Data brokers combine these records into profiles and sell access to them: to advertisers, insurers, political campaigns and others. It is a huge business. Mozilla puts the industry at about 264 billion dollars in 2023, growing to 471 billion by 2032. Some estimates say up to 5,000 brokers operate worldwide. That is why so many apps can be free: your data pays for them.”
