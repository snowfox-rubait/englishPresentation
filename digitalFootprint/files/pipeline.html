Key Concept: Identity Resolution

Data brokers and ad platforms do not treat your phone, laptop, home IP, and personal email as separate entities. Identity resolution algorithms merge these fragmented identifiers into a single persistent profile, associating every click across work, personal, and mobile devices.

SPEAKER NOTES (Speaker B / 388 takes over · 50s) “Thank you. Now, how does fragmented tracking data become a weapon of persuasion? It moves through a six-stage pipeline. Step 1: Raw data is collected via trackers, SDKs, and cookies. Step 2: Identity Resolution merges disparate identifiers—your work laptop, home Wi-Fi, personal phone, and email aliases become tied to one persistent individual. Step 3: Profiles are synthesized. Step 4: Social graphs map who you talk to and how frequently. Step 5: Machine learning models predict your likely decisions. Step 6: Platforms act—delivering hyper-targeted ads, adjusting insurance quotes, or re-ranking your newsfeed. The data isn't just stored; it is continually analyzed to predict what you will do next.”
