01 · THE FOUNDATION

What is a digital footprint?

ACTIVE

What you consciously share

  • Photos, videos, stories, and status updates
  • Comments, forum posts, and public reviews
  • Accounts and profiles you manually register
  • Forms, survey responses, and customer chats

PASSIVE

What is collected in the background

  • Real-time GPS location and Wi-Fi telemetry
  • Search queries, click sequences, and referrals
  • Hardware specs, battery status, and OS build
  • Browser canvas fingerprints and ad cookies

It’s not a single trace. It’s a longitudinal record that grows for years.

SPEAKER NOTES (Speaker A · 45s) “Your footprint divides into two distinct categories. First is the active footprint: the photos you upload, status updates, reviews, and messages. This is what most people think of when they hear 'digital footprint'. You have agency over it. Second—and far larger—is the passive footprint. This is the telemetry gathered while you simply exist online: your IP address, device specs, browsing duration, and movement patterns. You never explicitly agree to it, and it happens entirely in the background. The key insight: a digital footprint isn't a snapshot in time. It is a continuous, longitudinal record that compounds over years.”
