05 · THE BUSINESS MODEL

The feed is built for them, not you

The product is your attention and predicted behavior. Advertisers are the paying customers.

Variable Rewards

Dopamine Slot Machine

Pull-to-refresh and algorithmic feeds deliver unpredictable rewards. You scroll because the next post *might* be interesting—the exact psychological trigger exploited in gambling machines.

Filter Bubbles & Outrage

Engagement Over Truth

Algorithms prioritize content that maximizes reaction time. Moral outrage and agreeable echo-chambers keep users engaged longer than balanced or nuanced reporting.

Vulnerability Targeting

Monetizing Low Points

Users scrolling late at night or during stressful periods spend significantly longer on screen. Documented studies reveal that vulnerable states generate peak advertising and scam profitability.

SPEAKER NOTES (Speaker B · 45s) “Why is the digital environment constructed this way? Because social feeds are not designed to inform you—they are engineered to capture your attention and sell access to your future behavior. Variable rewards turn your feed into a casino slot machine. Filter bubbles feed confirmation bias and outrage because outrage generates comments and shares. And most dangerously, when users are lonely, exhausted, or anxious, their defense mechanisms drop and their scroll times soar—making them the most profitable targets for advertising and scams. You are not the customer; you are the inventory being calibrated.”
